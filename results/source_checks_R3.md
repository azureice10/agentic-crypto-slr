## Positioning and requirements

The live [JAIR submission guidance](https://www.jair.org/index.php/jair/about/submissions) requires the completed reproducibility checklist in the main submission and substantive AI relevance; a literature catalogue without new insight is a stated rejection risk. The [survey page](https://www.jair.org/index.php/jair/surveypapers) applies regular research standards to surveys. The 25–30-page target is the authors' editorial preference, not a verified journal page limit. Essential evidence and methodological qualifications therefore remain in the main article.

Positioning was checked against [Plaat et al.'s JAIR record](https://www.jair.org/index.php/jair/article/view/18675) and [earlier arXiv text](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.23037v2), [Lin et al.'s JAIR PDF](https://www.jair.org/index.php/jair/article/download/17654/27131), [Corso et al.'s JAIR PDF](https://www.jair.org/index.php/jair/article/download/12716/26727), and the [Belief–Intention–Permission SoK](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.06914v2). These are targeted comparisons, not a second exhaustive search of surveys. Established foundations are acknowledged through [Saltzer and Schroeder](https://web.mit.edu/Saltzer/www/publications/protection/Basic.html) and [Saltzer, Reed and Clark](https://web.mit.edu/Saltzer/www/publications/endtoend/endtoend.pdf).

## What this ledger does not certify

It does not validate every bibliography entry, source hash, claim code or primary result. It does not create new human sign-off for the canonical extraction. A future canonical correction should use an explicit claim-level amendment and revised release identifiers; it should not overwrite the historical row silently.
